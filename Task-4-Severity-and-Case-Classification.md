## Key Concepts

### 1. Seriousness

An adverse event is considered **serious** if it results in any of the following:

- Death
- Life-threatening condition
- Hospitalization or prolongation of hospitalization
- Persistent or significant disability/incapacity
- Congenital anomaly/birth defect
- Other medically important condition requiring medical intervention to prevent serious outcomes

If none of these criteria are met, the event is generally considered **non-serious**.

---

### 2. Severity

Severity describes the **intensity of the adverse event**.

Common categories include:

- Mild
- Moderate
- Severe

> **Important:** Severity and seriousness are not the same.

For example, a severe headache may be non-serious if it does not meet any seriousness criteria.

---

### 3. Expectedness

Expectedness is assessed by determining whether the adverse reaction is consistent with the available product information, such as the prescribing information or reference safety information.

- **Expected:** The reaction is consistent with the known safety information.
- **Unexpected:** The nature, severity, or specificity of the reaction is not consistent with the available safety information.

## Case 1 – Amoxicillin

**Adverse Reaction:** Skin rash and itching

### Classification

- **Seriousness:** Non-serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected

### Rationale

The patient experienced skin rash and itching without any reported hospitalization, life-threatening condition, disability, or other serious outcome. Therefore, the reaction is classified as **non-serious**.

The reaction is considered **expected** when it is consistent with the known safety information for amoxicillin.

---

## Case 2 – Ibuprofen

**Adverse Reaction:** Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization

### Classification

- **Seriousness:** Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected

### Rationale

The patient required hospitalization due to gastrointestinal bleeding. Hospitalization is one of the regulatory seriousness criteria. Therefore, the case is classified as **serious**.

The reaction is classified as **expected** when it is consistent with the available product safety information.

---

## Case 3 – Metformin

**Adverse Reaction:** Lactic acidosis

### Classification

- **Seriousness:** Serious
- **Expectedness:** Expected

### Rationale

Lactic acidosis is a clinically significant adverse reaction that can be life-threatening and requires urgent medical management. Therefore, the case is classified as **serious**.

It is considered **expected** when the reaction is consistent with the known safety information for metformin.
