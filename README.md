# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 1
## ADR Identification Basics

### Internship
Pharmacovigilance Internship at VirtualWorks Lab by Noivrn

### Task
ADR Identification Basics

### Objective
To develop a foundational understanding of Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs), their classification, assessment, and reporting within the field of pharmacovigilance.

### Key Learning Areas

#### 1. Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs)
An Adverse Drug Reaction is a harmful and unintended response associated with the use of a medicinal product at normally used doses.

#### 2. Classification of ADRs
The tutorial covered different categories of ADRs, including:
- Pharmacological
- Pathological
- Genotoxic
- Allergic
- Idiosyncratic
- Pharmacogenetic
- Teratogenic

#### 3. Hypersensitivity Reactions
The four major types of hypersensitivity reactions were reviewed:
- Type I – Immediate hypersensitivity
- Type II – Cytotoxic hypersensitivity
- Type III – Immune-complex mediated hypersensitivity
- Type IV – Delayed, T-cell mediated hypersensitivity

#### 4. Teratogenic Drug Categories
The pregnancy risk categories A, B, C, D and X were reviewed, with emphasis on understanding potential fetal risks associated with medicinal products.

#### 5. Causality Assessment
The tutorial introduced important approaches used to assess the relationship between a suspected drug and an adverse event:
- Bradford Hill Criteria
- WHO-UMC Causality Assessment
- Naranjo Algorithm

#### 6. ADR Reporting
The learning module covered ADR reporting through:
- CDSCO ADR reporting forms
- ADR PvPI mobile application

#### 7. Pharmacovigilance
The tutorial provided an introduction to pharmacovigilance and its role in the detection, assessment, understanding and prevention of adverse effects and other drug-related problems.

#### 8. Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI)
The module introduced the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India and its role in strengthening adverse drug reaction monitoring and improving medicine safety in India.

### Key Takeaway

This learning activity helped strengthen my understanding of the fundamental steps involved in identifying, assessing and reporting potential adverse drug reactions. It also highlighted the importance of pharmacovigilance in promoting patient safety and improving the safe use of medicines.
