# ADR Classification

The case is identified in the exercise as a:

## Type B ADR – Bizarre

Type B reactions are generally **unpredictable** and are not related to the usual pharmacological action of the drug.

The generalized itching and skin rash following amoxicillin exposure are consistent with a possible hypersensitivity-type reaction.

| Event | Date |

# Common Documentation Principles

While completing an ADR report:

- Record information accurately and objectively.
- Do not invent missing information.
- Document dates clearly.
- Clearly identify the suspected medication.
- Record the reaction and outcome.
- Include relevant concomitant medications.
- Maintain patient confidentiality.
- Distinguish information provided in the case from information that still requires follow-up.

---

# Confidentiality

Patient identity must be kept strictly confidential.

An ADR report is submitted for safety monitoring and does not by itself constitute an admission that a healthcare professional, manufacturer, or medicinal product caused or contributed to the reaction.
